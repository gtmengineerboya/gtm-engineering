# GTM Fundamentals

## What is GTM?

GTM means Go-To-Market.

It is the process used to take a product to the target market
and generate customers and revenue.

---

## Lead

A potential customer or prospect entering the GTM system.

---

## Prospect

A potential customer being considered for engagement.

---

## MQL

Marketing Qualified Lead.

A lead that meets defined marketing qualification criteria.

---

## SQL

Sales Qualified Lead.

A lead that has been qualified for sales engagement.

---

## Opportunity

A qualified potential sales transaction.

---

## Customer

A prospect that has purchased the product or service.

---

## GTM Funnel

Lead
↓
MQL
↓
SQL
↓
Opportunity
↓
Customer

---

## ICP

Ideal Customer Profile.

The characteristics of companies that are a good fit
for the product.

---

## Buyer Persona

The type of person or role inside the target company
that is relevant to the buying process.

---

## Lead Scoring

Assigning points to leads based on defined qualification
criteria.

Example:

Industry = 25
Company Size = 20
Location = 15
Job Title = 20
Technology = 10
Buying Signal = 10

Maximum Score = 100

---

## Lead Enrichment

Adding additional information to an existing lead record.

Example:

Company
→ Website
→ Industry
→ Employees
→ Technology
→ Location
→ Contact
→ Job Title

---

## Pipeline

The stages through which a lead progresses toward becoming
a customer.

---

## Conversion Rate

Percentage of records that move from one stage to another.

Formula:

Conversion Rate =
(Number entering next stage / Number entering previous stage)
× 100

---

## CAC

Customer Acquisition Cost.

The cost associated with acquiring a customer.

---

## LTV

Customer Lifetime Value.

The value generated by a customer over the customer relationship.

---

## MRR

Monthly Recurring Revenue.

---

## ARR

Annual Recurring Revenue.

---

## Inbound

Potential customers come to the company.

---

## Outbound

The company proactively identifies and contacts potential
customers.
